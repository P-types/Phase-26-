## Working title

What I learned by measuring retrieval before generating an answer

## Core idea

A retrieval-augmented answer depends on finding the right evidence first. This week's local exercise used a small synthetic FAQ set and three fictional manuals so the retrieval steps could be inspected without sending real records to a provider.

## Three ideas to explain

1. Chunking changes which facts arrive together.
2. Dense and lexical retrieval fail in different ways; a hybrid stage can combine their signals.
3. Citations and abstention make unsupported answers easier to spot, but proxy metrics do not replace human review or a model-based evaluation.
